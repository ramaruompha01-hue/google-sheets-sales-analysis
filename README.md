# Google Sheets Sales Analysis 📗

## 📌 Project Overview
Analysed Superstore sales data using Google Sheets to uncover key business 
insights through pivot tables and data visualisations across 4 focus areas.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Google Sheets
- Pivot Tables
- Charts & Visualisations

## 📂 Dataset
- Source: Kaggle — Superstore Sales Dataset
- 9,800 rows of transactional sales data

## 📊 Analysis Completed

### Pivot Table 1 — Sales by Category
| Category | Total Sales |
|---|---|
| Technology | Highest |
| Furniture | Second |
| Office Supplies | Third |

💡 **Insight:** Technology leads all categories in total revenue showing 
strong demand for tech products across all regions.

### Pivot Table 2 — Sales by Region
| Region | Performance |
|---|---|
| West | Highest |
| East | Second |
| Central | Lowest |

💡 **Insight:** West region generates the highest total sales while Central 
represents the biggest growth opportunity.

### Pivot Table 3 — Top 10 Customers
💡 **Insight:** Sean Miller leads at $25,043 with all top 10 customers 
spending above $12,000 showing a strong loyal customer base.

### Pivot Table 4 — Monthly Sales Trend
💡 **Insight:** Sales consistently peak in November and December every year 
confirming strong seasonal buying patterns driven by holiday spending.

## ✅ Key Findings
- Technology is the highest revenue category
- West region lead
s in total sales
- Sales peak at end of year every year
- A small group of loyal customers drive significant revenue
